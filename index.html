How to play.

01

Choose a square One team picks an unclaimed square. With gravity enabled, the piece falls to the lowest available space in the column.

02

Answer the question A random question appears. The teacher can reveal the answer or choose another question.

03

Claim the square The teacher awards the square to the correct team. Neither answered? Skip, and the square remains open.

04

Connect to win Make a line horizontally, vertically or diagonally. You need four in a row, or three on the smallest grid.

Questions are random, not tied to particular squares. After every question has appeared, the bank reshuffles and starts again, just like Flippity Connecto.
